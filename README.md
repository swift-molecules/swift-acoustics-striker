# swift-acoustics-striker

Use this package when something is struck by a mass through a felt that stiffens as it is pressed: a drumstick, a mallet, a hammer. Start from `Acoustics.Striker`, a `Mass` and a swift-contact `Contact`; swift-acoustics-resonator strikes its resonances with one.

## Laws

- A striker is its `mass`, what moves with it, and its `contact`, what it presses with: no force until pressed, then F₀ (δ / δ₀)^α, with the energy that stores, as swift-contact gives them. It holds no law of its own.
